# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially:

- SIEM translations (SPL, Sentinel KQL, QRadar AQL, Chronicle YARA-L)
- New Sigma rules for the `ai.*` schema
- Corrections to framework mappings (explain your rationale)

## Ground rules
1. **No client data.** Hostnames, IPs, users and screenshots must be synthetic and watermarked.
2. **No copyrighted standard text.** Describe topics in your own words and link to the publisher.
3. Mappings must say what a SIEM can and cannot prove. Overclaiming is a bug.
4. Keep field names ECS-aligned; use the `ai.*` namespace for AI telemetry.

Open an issue first for large changes.
